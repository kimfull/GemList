## 🇹🇼 繁體中文 (Traditional Chinese)

### 🚀 什麼是 GemList？
Google Gemini 很聰明，但有時候找之前的神回覆就像在大海撈針，對吧？ GemList 是一個特製的 Chrome 擴充功能，為你的 Gemini 頁面無縫注入一個「強大且優雅的側邊欄」。

它不僅是一個摘要目錄，更是你的 AI 筆記總管。不用再無止盡地一直往上滾動，不用再迷失在冗長的對話海中！

### ✨ 殺手級功能
- 📌 **釘選最愛 (Pin & Save)**：看到超棒的回答？釘選起來！它會安全地留在側邊欄頂部，就像你專屬的知識便利貼。
- 📑 **智慧目錄 (Smart ToC)**：自動萃取使用者與 Gemini 的對話精華，建立清晰的大綱結構，點擊瞬間直達目標。
- 📋 **一鍵複製 (One-Click Copy)**：完美的 Markdown 格式提取。一鍵複製整段對話到你的筆記軟體，連最討厭的隱藏無障礙文字（如「你說了」）都幫你過濾得乾乾淨淨。
- 🪶 **極致輕量 (Ultra-Lightweight)**：純原生 JavaScript 打造，沒有肥大的前端框架，安靜地在背景運作，像忍者一樣輕盈。

## 🇬🇧 English

### 🚀 What is GemList?
Google Gemini is brilliant, but scrolling through endless chat history to find that *one* specific brilliant answer? Not so much. Enter **GemList**.

GemList is a bespoke Chrome Extension that seamlessly injects a "powerful and elegant sidebar" directly into your Gemini interface. It's not just a table of contents; it's your personal AI conversation manager. Stop scrolling endlessly and start organizing efficiently!

### ✨ Killer Features
- 📌 **Pin & Save**: Found a golden response? Pin it! It stays securely at the top of your sidebar, acting as your personal knowledge sticky note.
- 📑 **Smart Table of Contents**: Automatically extracts the essence of your conversation with Gemini, creating a clear, clickable outline. Teleport straight to the message you need.
- 📋 **One-Click Copy**: Flawless Markdown extraction. Copy an entire interaction round to your clipboard fully formatted. We even scrub out those annoying hidden screen-reader texts like "You said"!
- 🪶 **Ultra-Lightweight**: Built with pure vanilla JavaScript. No bloated dependencies or frameworks. It runs silently and swiftly in the background—like a ninja.
